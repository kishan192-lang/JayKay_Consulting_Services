Tax & Compliance Services

Rigorous tax planning, meticulous reconciliations, and timely return filings under current tax legislation.

GST Registration & Return Filing

Comprehensive assistance with new GST registration, amendment of core/non-core fields, preparation and filing of monthly GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and annual GSTR-9 returns with meticulous GSTR-2B input tax credit reconciliation.

Income Tax Return (ITR) Filing

Filing of Income Tax Returns for individuals, salaried employees, HUFs, partnership firms, professionals, and companies. Thorough reconciliation with Form 26AS, AIS, and TIS to avoid defective return notices.

TDS / TCS Filings & Statements

Assistance with monthly TDS challan generation (Challan 281), quarterly e-TDS return filing (Form 24Q for salaries, Form 26Q for payments, Form 27Q for NRI payments), and timely generation of Form 16 / 16A certificates.

TDS on Sale / Purchase of Property

End-to-end guidance for property buyers on Section 194-IA compliance, generation of Form 26QB challan-cum-statement, and issuance of Form 16B certificate to sellers.

Important Tax Compliance Advisory: Tax statutes, filing forms, threshold limits, and exemption rules are subject to official regulatory amendments. All advisory is rendered in strict accordance with the current Finance Act and notifications applicable for the relevant assessment year.

Home • FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our service retainers, onboarding process, documents, and confidentiality standards.

What specific services does JayKay Consulting Services provide?

We provide comprehensive computerized accounting & bookkeeping, payroll processing, MIS and cash-flow reporting, GST registration & return filings, Income Tax (ITR) filings, TDS/TCS quarterly compliance, new business entity registrations, and senior-level corporate finance consulting.

Can your services be customized for my exact business size and budget?

Yes, absolutely. We recognize that a freelance consultant has very different requirements from an established manufacturing MSME. We configure our monthly retainer and turnaround timelines strictly around your volume of transactions and statutory obligations.

How does the monthly accounting & document collection process work?

We establish a streamlined document sharing workflow. You can share your purchase invoices, sales registers, bank statements, and expense bills securely via email, cloud drive, or physical pick-up. We record entries in Tally ERP, reconcile bank ledgers, check GST input credits, and provide monthly summaries.

Do you assist with GSTR-2B reconciliation to prevent input tax credit loss?

Yes. A critical component of our GST service is ongoing supplier invoice matching against GSTR-2B. We identify missing vendor invoices promptly so you can claim 100% eligible input tax credit without risking scrutiny or interest charges.

How do you handle confidential business data and employee payroll records?

Confidentiality is our cardinal rule. All client records, bank statements, and employee wage details are stored in encrypted, password-protected digital archives with strict access limitations. We gladly execute formal Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to onboarding.

How can I initiate an introductory consultation?

You can phone our Sholinganallur office at +91 97899 99834, email us directly, or send an enquiry via our website or WhatsApp. We will schedule a direct consultation with Founder & Principal Consultant Jayakumar Kachapeshwaran.
